## Billing & Payments FAQs

---

### Can I edit or delete a paid invoice?

**No.** Once an invoice is marked **Paid**, it is locked for audit integrity. You cannot edit or delete it.

If you made an error on a paid invoice, use **Credit Note** (available from the invoice page) to reverse the incorrect amount and create a corrected invoice.

---

### A patient paid partially. How do I record the rest later?

1. Go to **Billing** → **Outstanding**.
2. Find the patient's partially paid invoice.
3. Click **Record Payment**.
4. Enter the amount received today and the payment mode.
5. The invoice updates automatically — it shows **Paid** once the full amount is collected.

---

### How do I apply a discount?

When generating an invoice, you will see a **Discount** field. Enter the amount (flat) or percentage. You will also be prompted to enter a **reason for discount** — this is mandatory and shows on reports.

Discounts cannot be applied after an invoice is saved as Paid. Apply them before saving.

---

### Can I issue a refund?

The DMS does not process automatic refunds (it is not a payment gateway). To record a refund:
1. Create a **Credit Note** on the paid invoice.
2. Note the refund amount and reason.
3. Record the actual money returned separately (cash/bank transfer done outside the system).
4. The credit note appears in the patient's financial history and the clinic's accounts.

---

### Where do I set up GST / tax on invoices?

Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Tax Settings**. You can configure:
- GST percentage (5%, 12%, 18% or custom)
- Whether tax is included in the line item price or added on top
- The clinic's GSTIN for display on invoices

Once configured, tax is applied automatically to all future invoices.

---

### Why does the invoice show "Billed" but the patient says they already paid?

The payment may not have been recorded in the system. Check:
1. Open the invoice — look at the **Payment History** section at the bottom.
2. If no payment is listed, record it now under **Record Payment**.
3. If a payment is listed, confirm the amount and mode match what the patient claims.

Never trust verbal confirmation alone — always check the invoice payment history.

---

### How do I send the invoice / receipt to the patient?

On any invoice, click **Send via WhatsApp**. The receipt is sent as a formatted message to the patient's registered phone number. A PDF download is also available via the **Download** button.

WhatsApp delivery requires the clinic's WhatsApp number to be connected (check Settings → WhatsApp).

---

### Can I set up recurring invoices for treatment plans that span multiple visits?

Not yet — each visit has its own invoice. For multi-visit treatment plans, the standard practice is to invoice after each visit for the work done that day.

---

### Where do I see all outstanding amounts across all patients?

Go to **Billing** → **Outstanding** tab. This lists every invoice that is unpaid or partially paid, grouped by patient, with the amount due and the invoice date. You can also export this as a CSV for follow-up.
