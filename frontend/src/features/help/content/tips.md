## Things to Keep in Mind

Quick reminders for the most common mistakes — save this page, read it once a week.

---

### Patients

- **Phone number = patient identity.** One phone number per patient, always. If a family of four visits the clinic, each member needs their own number — even if the same parent pays for all.
- **Search before you register.** Never create a new patient record without searching first. Duplicate patients mean split treatment history and messy invoices.
- **Collect the email at registration, not later.** It takes 5 seconds to type, and without it you can't send digital invoices or reports.
- **Medical history and allergies up front.** Ask before the patient sits in the chair. "Any allergies?" is not a question for the middle of an extraction.

---

### Appointments

- **Green slot = available. Grey = taken.** If it's grey, it's not available — no exceptions. Coordinate with the provider offline if the patient insists.
- **Pick the right treatment type.** A consultation slot is shorter than an RCT slot. Picking the wrong type means the next patient's appointment runs over.
- **Mark the appointment status when the patient leaves.** Completed / No-show / Cancelled. If you don't update it, your appointment reports will be wrong.
- **No-show should be noted, not just ignored.** A patient who no-shows twice without notice needs a flag — it helps the front desk decide whether to hold the slot or overbook next time.

---

### Clinical Records

- **Clinical notes are permanent.** There is no "undo" on clinical notes. Think before you type. Correct errors by adding a dated correction note — never try to work around the system.
- **Record treatment the same day it happens.** Memory fades. A visit recorded 3 days later is a guess.
- **Use the Treatment Plan field.** If treatment needs 3 visits, write the whole plan in visit 1. Do not rely on memory or sticky notes.
- **Attach X-rays and referral letters to the visit, not the patient.** This keeps the timeline clean — you always know which X-ray belongs to which visit.

---

### Billing

- **Invoice after every visit, not "at the end of treatment."** Waiting accumulates a large outstanding bill that patients push back on. Smaller, per-visit invoices are easier to collect.
- **Billed ≠ Collected.** ₹1L billed this month and ₹1L collected this month are two very different things. Check both numbers in Analytics.
- **Outstanding tab every morning.** Spend 5 minutes on it at the start of each day. Calling a patient 2 days after the visit is far easier than calling them 2 months later.
- **Discounts need a reason, always.** Even if it feels obvious. Future-you (or your accountant) will thank present-you.

---

### WhatsApp Automation

- **Test every flow on your own number before activating.** A broken reminder sent to 200 patients cannot be unsent.
- **One active flow per trigger type.** Two "Appointment Booked" flows = every patient gets two messages. Check for duplicate active flows when something seems like it's firing twice.
- **Session drops happen.** If the clinic's WhatsApp shows "Disconnected," rescan the QR immediately — especially after a weekend or public holiday when the phone may have restarted.
- **Keep message templates short and service-focused.** Promotional language in the first message to a patient who hasn't messaged you in 24 hours will get flagged by WhatsApp. Keep it factual and clinic-related.

---

### General Habits

- **Save before you navigate away.** The browser doesn't ask "are you sure?" — it just leaves. Click Save.
- **Log in with your own account, always.** Shared accounts break audit trails. If a record was created by the wrong person, it creates compliance problems.
- **Use Chrome for the best experience.** Some features (especially the tooth chart and the visual flow builder) behave differently in Firefox or Edge. Chrome is the recommended browser.
- **If something looks wrong, hard-refresh first.** `Ctrl + Shift + R`. This fixes 80% of display glitches without needing to contact support.
- **Check your role permissions before raising a support ticket.** If you can't see a page, it might be role access, not a bug. Ask your admin first.
