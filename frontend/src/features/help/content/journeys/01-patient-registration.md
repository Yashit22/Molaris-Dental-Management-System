## How to Register a New Patient

Use this guide the first time a patient walks in. A patient must exist in the system before you can book an appointment or record any treatment.

---

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Patients**.
2. Click the **+ Add Patient** button (top-right of the list).
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Full Name** — use the patient's legal name as on their ID.
   - **Phone Number** — this is the unique identifier for every patient. No two patients can share the same number.
   - **Date of Birth** — used to auto-calculate age and for medical reports.
   - **Gender** — Male / Female / Other.
4. Fill in optional but recommended fields:
   - **Email** — for sending digital invoices and appointment reminders.
   - **Address** — useful for lab deliveries and correspondence.
   - **Medical History / Allergies** — flag any allergies (especially to anaesthesia or antibiotics) before the first treatment.
5. Click **Save Patient**.
6. The system assigns a unique **Patient ID** automatically. Note it down or share it with the patient for future reference.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **Phone number is the single source of truth.** If two family members visit the same clinic, they must be registered with different phone numbers. Never share a phone number between patients — it will cause appointment and invoice conflicts.

> **Double-check for duplicates before registering.** Search the patient's phone number first. If a record already exists, open it and update it — do not create a duplicate.

> **Medical history saves time in treatment.** Ask the patient about allergies and ongoing medications during registration, not during treatment. It is already too late once you are in the chair.

> **Incomplete registrations slow down billing.** If email is missing, digital invoices cannot be sent. Try to collect it upfront.

---

### What Happens Next

Once a patient is registered, you can:
- [Book an Appointment](02-book-appointment.md) for them immediately.
- Add clinical notes and treatment history under their profile.
- Generate invoices linked to their account.
