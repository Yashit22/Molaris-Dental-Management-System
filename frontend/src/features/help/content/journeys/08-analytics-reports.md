## Analytics & Reports

Use the Analytics section to understand clinic performance — revenue, appointments, patient growth, and outstanding collections — without any manual spreadsheet work.

---

### Key Reports Available

#### 1. Revenue Report
- **What it shows:** Total billed vs. total collected by day / week / month.
- **How to use:** Set the date range → view the bar chart or table → export to CSV for your accountant.
- **Billed ≠ Collected.** Billed is what was invoiced; Collected is what was actually paid. The gap is your outstanding balance.

#### 2. Appointment Report
- **What it shows:** Total appointments, completed vs. no-show vs. cancelled, by provider or treatment type.
- **How to use:** Filter by provider to see individual dentist productivity. High no-show rate on a specific day may indicate the reminder automation is not working.

#### 3. Patient Report
- **What it shows:** New patients registered vs. returning patients, by time period.
- **How to use:** Track growth month-on-month. A declining new-patient rate is an early signal to investigate marketing or referral sources.

#### 4. Outstanding Collections
- **What it shows:** All partially paid or unpaid invoices, aged by days outstanding.
- **How to use:** Focus on the 30+ day outstanding bucket first — these are the most at risk of never being collected.

#### 5. Treatment Report
- **What it shows:** Which treatments were performed most, revenue per treatment type, top services by volume.
- **How to use:** Identify your most profitable treatments and whether the chair time aligns with revenue.

---

### How to Use the Dashboard

1. Go to **Analytics** in the sidebar.
2. Set the **Date Range** (today / this week / this month / custom).
3. Use the top-level cards for a quick snapshot: Total Revenue, Appointments, New Patients, Outstanding.
4. Scroll down to the charts for trends over time.
5. Click **Export** on any report to download as CSV.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **Always set a date range before drawing conclusions.** Default view may show all-time data, which can look impressive but isn't useful for daily decision-making.

> **Billed vs. Collected is the most important distinction.** A clinic that bills ₹5L a month but collects ₹3.5L has a collections problem, not a revenue problem. Watch both numbers.

> **No-show rate above 10% needs attention.** Check whether appointment reminders are firing (WhatsApp automation), and whether the clinic is calling patients the day before.

> **Treatment mix tells you where to invest.** If 70% of revenue comes from 2 treatment types, specialise your marketing and training there rather than spreading thin.

> **Export data to Excel/CSV for month-end review.** Share it with your accountant as-is — the format is clean and ready to use.
