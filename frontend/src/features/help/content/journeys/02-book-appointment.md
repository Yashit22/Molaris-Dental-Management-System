## How to Book an Appointment

Appointments are the starting point of every clinic visit. You can book from the Appointments page or directly from a patient's profile.

---

### Steps

1. In the left sidebar, click **Appointments**.
2. Click **+ New Appointment**.
3. **Find the patient** — search by name or phone number.
   - If the patient is not in the list, [register them first](01-patient-registration.md) before proceeding.
4. **Select the Provider** — choose the dentist or staff member who will conduct the appointment.
5. **Pick the Date and Time** — available slots are shown in green; already-booked slots are greyed out.
6. **Select the Service / Treatment Type** — e.g. Consultation, Cleaning, RCT, Extraction. This sets the default duration.
7. Add an optional **Note** (e.g. "Patient anxious about injections", "Bring X-ray from last visit").
8. Click **Confirm Appointment**.
9. The patient receives a WhatsApp reminder automatically (if the clinic's WhatsApp automation is active).

---

### Rescheduling an Appointment

1. Open **Appointments** and find the existing booking.
2. Click the appointment → select **Reschedule**.
3. Pick the new date/time and confirm.
4. The patient gets an updated reminder.

### Cancelling an Appointment

1. Open the appointment → click **Cancel**.
2. Add a cancellation reason (optional but helpful for reporting).
3. The slot is freed immediately for others to book.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **Always search before booking.** Confirm the patient record exists and that the phone number is correct. A booking linked to the wrong patient creates billing chaos.

> **Slot colour is live.** Grey slots are taken — do not try to double-book. If a patient insists on a specific time, ask the provider to confirm offline first.

> **The treatment type sets the duration.** A "Consultation" slot is shorter than an "RCT" slot. Choosing the wrong type means the next patient's slot runs into this one.

> **WhatsApp reminders only fire if automation is on.** If the patient says they didn't get a reminder, check the WhatsApp Settings page to ensure the flow is active and the clinic's number is connected.
