## Inventory Management

Track dental materials and consumables so the clinic never runs out mid-treatment.

---

### Steps — Adding a New Item

1. Go to **Inventory** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ Add Item**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Item Name** (e.g. "GIC Luting Cement", "3M Bracket Kit")
   - **Unit** (pieces, boxes, ml, grams)
   - **Opening Stock** — current quantity on hand
   - **Reorder Level** — quantity at which you want an alert (e.g. alert when stock drops to 5 boxes)
   - **Unit Cost** — cost per unit for valuation reports
4. Click **Save**.

### Steps — Recording a Purchase (Stock In)

1. Open the item → click **+ Add Purchase**.
2. Enter: Quantity received, Supplier name, Invoice number, Date, Cost.
3. Stock level updates automatically.

### Steps — Recording Usage (Stock Out)

Usage can be logged manually or linked to a treatment visit.
1. Open the item → click **Record Usage**.
2. Enter quantity used and optionally link it to a patient visit.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **Set reorder levels before you need them.** An alert at 0 stock is useless — set it at 1–2 weeks of usage so you have time to reorder.

> **Link usage to visits when possible.** It gives you a cost-per-patient report over time and helps identify which treatments consume the most material.

> **Regular stock audits matter.** Do a physical count at the end of each month and reconcile with the system. Discrepancies usually point to unlogged usage.

> **Expensive items need their own line.** Implants, zirconia blocks, and other high-value items should each have individual inventory entries — not bundled under a generic category.
