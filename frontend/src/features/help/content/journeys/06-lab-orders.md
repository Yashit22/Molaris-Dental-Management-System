## Lab Orders

Raise a lab order whenever work needs to go to an external dental lab — crowns, bridges, dentures, aligners, night guards, etc.

---

### Steps — Creating a Lab Order

1. Open the patient's visit record (where the impression was taken).
2. Click **+ Lab Order**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Lab Name** — select from your registered labs, or type a new one.
   - **Work Type** — Crown / Bridge / Denture / Aligner / Retainer / Other.
   - **Shade / Specifications** — add the shade code (e.g. A2), material (e.g. Zirconia), and any special instructions.
   - **Expected Delivery Date** — agree on this with the lab before saving.
4. Attach the prescription slip or shade guide photo if available.
5. Click **Send to Lab** (saves the order and marks it as **Sent**).

### Steps — Updating Lab Order Status

As the work progresses, update the status:
- **Sent** → work dispatched to lab
- **In Progress** → lab has acknowledged
- **Ready for Trial** → try-in stage (for crowns/dentures)
- **Received** → work back at the clinic, ready to fit
- **Fitted** → cemented/delivered to patient

### Steps — Receiving a Lab Order

1. Go to **Lab Orders** in the sidebar.
2. Find the order → click **Mark Received**.
3. Enter the actual delivery date and any notes (e.g. shade discrepancy, minor adjustments needed).
4. The order links back to the patient's visit automatically.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **Create the lab order the same day you take the impression.** Waiting causes it to get forgotten, and the lab does not have your specifications in writing.

> **Always confirm the delivery date with the lab before promising the patient.** Lab delays are the most common cause of patient complaints in restorative dentistry.

> **Shade and material specifications must be exact.** A missing shade code means the lab will call back or, worse, guess. Write it in, always.

> **Lab costs can be added to the patient's invoice.** Once the order is received and fitted, add the lab fee as a line item when generating or updating the invoice.
