## Invoicing & Billing

Generate an invoice once treatment is complete for a visit. An invoice records what was charged, what was paid, and what remains outstanding.

---

### Steps

1. Open the patient's completed visit record (from **Patients** → patient profile → the visit).
2. Click **Generate Invoice**.
3. The system pre-fills the services performed in that visit. **Add any additional line items** (e.g. consumables, X-ray charges) using **+ Add Item**.
4. For each line item, confirm:
   - **Service name**
   - **Quantity**
   - **Rate** (pulled from your service catalogue; edit if needed for this case)
5. Apply a **Discount** if applicable — percentage or flat amount. Add a reason (required for audit trail).
6. Review the **Total Amount**.
7. Under **Payment Received**, enter the amount the patient is paying today and the payment mode: **Cash / Card / UPI / Online Transfer**.
8. If the patient is paying the full amount, the invoice closes as **Paid**.
9. If the patient pays partially, the invoice shows **Partially Paid** and the balance appears as **Outstanding**.
10. Click **Save & Generate Invoice**.
11. A PDF receipt is available to download and is sent to the patient via WhatsApp automatically (if automation is active).

---

### Collecting Outstanding Payments

1. Go to **Billing** → **Outstanding** tab.
2. Find the patient — filter by name, date, or amount.
3. Click **Record Payment** → enter the amount received and mode → Save.
4. The invoice updates to **Paid** once the full amount is collected.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **One invoice per visit is the clean practice.** Avoid bundling multiple visit charges into one invoice — it makes reports and audits confusing.

> **Partial payment is fine, but follow up.** The Outstanding tab is your daily collection list. Review it every morning.

> **Discounts need a reason.** Even for friends or family, add a note. This protects the clinic during audits.

> **Do not edit a Paid invoice.** If you made an error on a paid invoice, use the **Credit Note** flow instead of modifying the original — it preserves the financial audit trail.

> **WhatsApp receipt only fires if the patient's phone is in the system and the automation is active.** Check the WhatsApp Settings page if receipts are not being delivered.
