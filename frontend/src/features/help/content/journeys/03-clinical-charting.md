## Clinical Charting & Treatment Records

Every visit should have a clinical record — treatment done, diagnosis, and any prescriptions. This is both a medical and legal requirement.

---

### Steps

1. Go to **Patients** and open the patient's profile.
2. Click **+ New Visit** (or open an existing appointment and click **Start Treatment**).
3. In the **Tooth Chart**, click the affected tooth to mark the treatment area.
   - Teeth are numbered using the **FDI two-digit system** (e.g. 11 = upper-right central incisor).
   - Click a tooth → choose the surface (Mesial, Distal, Occlusal, Buccal, Lingual) → select the condition (Caries, Fracture, Missing, etc.).
4. Under **Diagnosis**, describe the clinical finding in free text.
5. Under **Treatment Performed**, record what was done today (e.g. "RCT — access opening, canal preparation up to #30").
6. Under **Treatment Plan**, note what still needs to be done in future visits.
7. Add a **Prescription** if medications were given — drug name, dosage, frequency, duration.
8. Add **Clinical Notes** for anything else (patient behaviour, radiograph findings, special instructions).
9. Click **Save Visit**.

---

### Things to Keep in Mind

> **Notes are permanent.** Once saved, clinical notes cannot be deleted — only appended. This is intentional for medico-legal compliance. Write accurately from the start.

> **Use the treatment plan field.** If treatment spans multiple visits (e.g. RCT has 3 stages), write the full plan on visit 1. Each subsequent visit records what was completed that day.

> **Prescriptions should match the visit.** If you prescribe antibiotics after an extraction, record it under that visit — not a generic note. It links to the patient's medication history correctly.

> **X-rays and documents can be attached.** Use the attachment option in the visit to upload radiographs or referral letters directly to that visit record.
